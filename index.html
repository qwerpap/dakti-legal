Dakti: ASL Sign Language · Last updated September 27, 2026

Need help with Dakti? Write to netkaktakto@gmail.com — include your iPhone model and what happened. In the app, Settings → Send feedback fills in the version for you.

The camera doesn't accept my letter

  • Hold the phone about a forearm away, with your hand in front of your chest and the palm toward the camera.
  • Use good, even light and a plain background.
  • Check the drawing: which fingers are straight, where the thumb goes. The hint line tells you which finger to change.
  • If you sign with your left hand, turn on "I sign with my left hand" in Settings.

Restore a purchase

Open the paywall or Settings and tap Restore. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

Cancel a subscription

Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Dakti → Cancel Subscription.
