Privacy Policy

Dakti: ASL Sign Language · Last updated September 27, 2026

Dakti is a practice app for the American Sign Language manual alphabet (fingerspelling). There are no accounts and no server that stores your practice. We don't sell or share your data.

Camera

When you practice, Dakti uses the front camera to find the points of your hand (knuckles and fingertips) with Apple's on-device Vision framework and compares their shape with the letter. Camera frames are processed in memory on your iPhone, are never recorded, saved or uploaded, and are discarded right away. You can turn camera access off at any time in iOS Settings; the drawings and reading practice still work.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Which letters the camera confirmed and when, your daily streak.
  • What you told us in onboarding: why you're learning, which hand you sign with, your daily goal, and your reminder time.

This is stored only in Dakti's own storage on your iPhone.

Reminders

The daily practice reminder and the note a day before a free trial ends are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. No push server is involved.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Dakti receives only the status of your purchase, never your payment details. We use a subscription-management service (Adapty) that receives an anonymous app-install identifier, purchase status and which onboarding step was shown, to validate purchases and measure how the paywall performs. It never receives camera frames or hand data.

Analytics and tracking

Dakti does not track you across apps or websites, does not use advertising identifiers and contains no third-party analytics beyond the subscription service above.

Deleting your data

Delete Dakti from your iPhone to remove everything it stored.

Children

Dakti is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
