Privacy Policy

What the camera sees, what stays on the phone, and what is shared.

The camera

Dakti opens the front camera only on practice screens, to find your hand and compare its shape with a letter. The analysis (Apple Vision hand tracking) runs on your iPhone. Frames are not recorded, saved, or sent to any server, ours or anyone else's. Camera access can be turned off in iOS Settings, Dakti; you can still read the letter guides without it.

Your progress

Which letters you have mastered, streaks, your daily goal, the hand you prefer and your reminder hour are saved in the app's local storage. Dakti has no accounts and no server holding any of it. Deleting the app removes it.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the daily practice reminder is scheduled on the phone at the hour you choose. No push server is involved.

Purchases

Apple handles payment. We use Adapty to confirm which plan is active, choose the offer to show and count anonymous paywall events. Adapty receives a random profile id, App Store receipt data, country, app version and device model. IP address collection is turned off. Your camera input and practice results are never part of what it receives.

Ads and tracking

No ads, no advertising identifier, no tracking across other apps or sites, and no selling of personal data.

If you write to us

The feedback button opens an email with the app version, iOS version and the random profile id; we use it only to reply.

Children

Dakti may be used by learners of any age under a parent's guidance, but we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone: the app collects none beyond the anonymous purchase data above.
