What Dakti is

A practice tool for the American Sign Language manual alphabet. The camera check compares hand shapes with reference poses and can be wrong: lighting, skin tone, camera angle and individual hand differences all affect it. It is not a certified ASL course, does not assess your signing for any official purpose, and does not replace learning from Deaf signers.

Free and Pro
