A practice tool

Dakti helps you practice the ASL manual alphabet. It is not a certified ASL course, not a translator and not a substitute for learning with Deaf teachers and the Deaf community. The camera check looks at the shape of your hand on your device and can miss details or accept a near miss.

Subscriptions

Dakti Pro is an auto-renewing subscription (weekly or yearly). Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel any time in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. If a free trial is offered, any unused part of it is forfeited when you buy a subscription.
